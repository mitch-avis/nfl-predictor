## Cells within tolerance but not bit-identical

- `qb_ratings`: `qb_epa_per_dropback`
- `qb_combined`: `qb_epa_per_dropback`

A passer's raw season EPA per dropback (`qb_epa_per_dropback`) is a season statistic shown beside
the rating, not a fit. There it is the season's passing EPA total less the spikes' EPA total, over
the dropback total, each summed in that project's row order; here it is the per-game dropback EPA
(passing less spikes) summed over the passer's games, so the last bit can differ.

No fitted value differs.
