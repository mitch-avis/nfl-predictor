## 6. "None unexplained" and the attribution logic

`attribute` (`parity.py:306-343`) marks a team-game explained only when some combination of the
pair's own allowed causes makes the adjusted value equal. Counts must be exactly equal; sums and
rates must agree within 1e-6. Nothing defaults to explained:

- Records start as `unexplained`.
- A pair with no causes cannot be explained.
- A cause whose evidence pattern fails fails the run.
- Any record left unexplained fails the run (`parity.py:573-576`).
- Every cause in `parity.yaml` has an implementation and back. An adversarial test confirms this.

Each mismatching pair has an attribution: `dropbacks`, `pass_epa_sum`, `pass_success_count`,
`scrimmage_epa_sum`, `early_down_pass_rate`, `first_downs` and `fumbles`, 25,564 in all.

The two new causes (`wiped_out_run_epa` and `unflagged_rush_attempt_epa`) are narrow rewrites of
single flags, not catch-alls. Adversarial tests confirm they leave non-target plays alone. The
widening of `kneels_and_spikes` (it now also sets the rush-attempt flag) is explained in entry 6.
