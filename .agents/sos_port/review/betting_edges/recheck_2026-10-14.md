## Adversarial tests

- Location: `/home/mitch/scratch/review_betting_edges/tests/test_review_recheck_betting_edges.py`,
  uncommitted.
- Result: 25 tests, **25 passed, 0 failed**. The earlier reviewer's file also passes, 24 of 24.
- The one warning is numpy's overflow in `floor_sigma.py:129` from the subnormal-sigma case.
  It is expected: the probability saturates to 0 and 1 as intended.

What they cover:

- **Huge finite sigma** (`1e300` and `sys.float_info.max`): accepted, and the spread
  probability is `0.5`.
- **Smallest subnormal sigma** (`5e-324`): accepted, and saturates to `0` and `1`.
- **Numpy infinite values** (`np.float64(inf)`, `np.float32(inf)`, `-np.inf`) and `+inf` with no
  spreads: all raise.
- **Numpy integer sigma** (`np.int64(13)`): accepted.
- **The run helper:**
  - an `inf` next to a finite value, in either order, gives `None`;
  - `[inf, NaN]` gives `None`;
  - all `-inf`, or `-inf` with `13.5`, gives `None`;
  - `[1e300, 1e300]` gives `1e300`, and integer `[13, 13]` gives `13.0`;
  - an empty column gives `None`.
- **Odd column types in the run helper:**
  - nullable `Float64` with NA gives `13.5`, and `[inf, NA]` gives `None`;
  - the object strings `"inf"`, and `"-inf"` with `"13.5"`, give `None`;
  - `["13.5", "oops"]` gives `13.5`;
  - float32 `inf` gives `None`.
  - The result is always a Python `float`.
- **End to end through the weekly run's report stage:** a `predictions.csv` with an all-`inf` or
  all-`-inf` `floor_sigma` column passes no sigma (`None`) to the report.
