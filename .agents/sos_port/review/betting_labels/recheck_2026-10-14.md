## The proposed CHANGELOG line

The draft is accurate, but it falls short of the precedent set by the `0.58.0` `betting_edges.py`
entry and the `0.57.1` `elo_qb_alt.py` entry:

- It gives no number and no game set. Those entries quote the governing figure and say "reviewed
  independently".
- It does not name the protocol written before any score, or that the script reads both seeds.
- "decision entry 3" has no path; it lives in `.agents/sos_port/decisions/betting_edges.md`.
- It leaves out "report-only" (nothing in the report changes).
- It leaves out the README section and the tests. Earlier entries also leave these out, so this
  is fine.
- It needs its own version, presumably `0.58.1`, set in `pyproject.toml` with `uv lock` and the
  CUDA sync (AGENTS.md), never `[Unreleased]`.

Suggested replacement, under `### Added`:

```markdown
- `.agents/sos_port/betting_labels.py` tests the betting report's confidence score and action
  label on the whole moneyline edge against the model's part beyond the spread (report-only), on
  both seeds of the adopted configuration's reference, and writes
  `.agents/sos_port/BETTING_LABELS.md` and `betting_labels.json`. Under its protocol, written
  before any score (`.agents/sos_port/protocols/betting_labels.md`), the labels stay on the whole
  edge: on the 815 games of the seasons with real moneylines (2022, 2024, 2025), the Brier score
  of the beyond-the-spread claim minus the model's is `-0.00036` (95% interval `-0.00198` to
  `+0.00135`), which contains zero (reviewed independently; decision entry 3 in
  `.agents/sos_port/decisions/betting_edges.md`).
```
